# Tool scout 2026-10-11

2 licence-safe candidates checked; model: gemini-3.8-flash.

## [openbmb/VoxCPM2](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/VoxCPM2)
- For: Kyun Hua (Hindi voiceover generation) and Orbitwire (English emotional narration)
- Why: Provides expressive, emotionally nuanced bilingual (Hindi and English) text-to-speech under an Apache-2.0 licence. Kyun Hua currently depends on Gemini TTS Sulafat and Orbitwire on Kokoro-82M. VoxCPM2 could provide distinct, authentic emotional delivery that elevates retention and helps bypass YPP inauthentic/generic voice flagging during human review, while reducing API dependency.
- Test: Benchmark a 45-second bilingual script (one Hindi explainer beat, one English dramatic beat) on a GitHub Actions 4-vCPU runner and Modal CPU container. Measure Real-Time Factor (RTF), memory footprint (< 16 GB RAM limit), and compare naturalness/clarity against Kokoro and Gemini Sulafat using UTMOS scoring.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 is commercial-safe. Architectural compute requirements may be too heavy for standard 4 vCPU runners without quantization or ONNX runtime, potentially requiring execution within Modal free credits.

## [meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct)
- For: Scam Autopsy (fact-checking and evidence extraction) and Orbitwire (script structurer/fact verification)
- Why: Runs reliably as a 4-bit GGUF (Q4_K_M) in llama.cpp under 5 GB RAM on 4 vCPUs. It offers proven JSON schema adherence and tool-use precision for automated claim verification and script formatting on the local runner, serving as an offline, zero-quota-risk fallback when Gemini Flash faces rate-limiting or network volatility.
- Test: Run a llama.cpp GGUF instance on a 4-vCPU GitHub Actions runner with a sample 500-word scam investigation source text. Validate structured JSON extraction of dates, claims, and verified citations within 30 seconds per run, checking output consistency across 5 iterations.
- Risk: Subject to the Llama 3.1 Community License (requires attribution and commercial compliance under 700M MAU). May trail newer Gemma models in Hindi linguistic nuances, so it should be confined to English claim extraction and structural validation.
