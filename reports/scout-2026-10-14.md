## [mistralai/Voxtral-Mini-4B-Realtime-2602](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Voxtral-Mini-4B-Realtime-2602)
- For: Kyah Hua and Orbitwire - Speech recognition / forced alignment for karaoke captions
- Why: Provides real-time speech recognition and forced alignment to generate dynamic karaoke captions on CPU runners, improving first-30-second retention and viewer engagement for Hindi and English Shorts.
- Test: Run a 5-minute audio clip through Voxtral-Mini on a local CPU runner, measure transcription accuracy and alignment timestamp latency compared to faster-whisper.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 licence, 1028 stars, active early 2026 update. Needs memory check to fit comfortably alongside other runner tasks on 16GB RAM.

## [speridlabs/iris-3b](https://github.com/speridlabs/iris-3b)
- For: Orbitwire and Atlas in Numbers - Monocular depth estimation for photo parallax
- Why: Offers pixel-space generation and general vision learning including fine-tuned monocular depth estimation, potentially creating smoother parallax effects from Wikimedia Commons photos to increase visual variety and subscriber conversion.
- Test: Process 5 test images through Iris-3b on a CPU runner, export depth maps, and render a short ffmpeg parallax clip to check frame generation time and visual depth quality.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 licence, 163 stars, updated October 2026. A 3B diffusion-based model running on CPU may be slower than dedicated small depth models; requires performance benchmarking.

## [JetBrains/Mellum2.1-12B-A2.5B-Thinking-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/JetBrains/Mellum2.1-12B-A2.5B-Thinking-GGUF)

- Why: A 12B thinking GGUF model that can run locally via llama.cpp on CPU runners to produce more rigorously researched, narrative-driven script drafts and fact-checks, lowering the risk of generic template content.
- Test: Load the GGUF model via llama.cpp, generate a 60-second Hindi/English script outline with fact-check citations, and measure token generation speed per second on CPU.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 licence, 80 stars, updated October 2026. Requires tuning thread counts and context sizes to fit within GitHub Actions 4 vCPU limits without timing out pipelines.
