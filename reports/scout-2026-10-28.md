# Tool scout 2026-10-28

9 licence-safe candidates checked; model: gemini-3.7-flash.

## [maya-research/maya2-native](https://huggingface.co/maya-research/maya2-native)
- For: Kahaani Chaos (dialogue narration) and Kyun Hua (Hindi explainer voiceover)
- Why: Provides native Hindi and English emotional speech synthesis. Moving Hindi voice generation to a local engine reduces dependence on Gemini TTS quotas and broadens vocal range to avoid repetitive synthetic voice flags during YPP review.
- Test: Run inference on a 30-second dramatic Hindi script excerpt on a 4 vCPU runner; measure real-time factor (RTF) and evaluate UTMOS22 naturalness against Sulafat.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 is fully commercial-safe. Requires verification of RAM consumption and CPU inference speed on GitHub Actions 4 vCPU runners.

## [ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc)
- For: Kyun Hua, Orbitwire, and Atlas in Numbers (karaoke caption alignment)
- Why: CTC-based alignment is non-autoregressive, delivering precise word-level subtitle boundaries much faster on CPU than faster-whisper, while eliminating the timestamp drifting and looping hallucinations that hurt Shorts watch retention.
- Test: Align a 45-second pre-rendered Short audio track against its source text on a 4 vCPU Linux runner; compare execution duration and millisecond accuracy against faster-whisper.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 license is fully clear. Requires validation of timestamp precision on Hinglish and Indian-accented English phonemes.

## [XHToken/Spark-X2.6-4B-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/XHToken/Spark-X2.6-4B-GGUF)
- For: Scam Autopsy, Kyun Hua, and Doctor drill (CI script linting, fact-check sanity, and CTA hooks)
- Why: A 4B quantized GGUF executes at low latency on 4 vCPU via llama.cpp without exceeding runner memory limits. It enables automated in-pipeline checks for hook retention, subscribe CTAs, and structural repetition without burning Gemini API rates.
- Test: Load the Q4_K_M GGUF in llama.cpp on a 4 vCPU runner, execute a script-linting prompt against 5 historical scripts, and verify completion time is under 20 seconds with valid JSON output.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 license. Smaller 4B parameter models can hallucinate if unconstrained; prompting must strictly demand extraction from provided context.

## [ACE-Step/acestep-v15-xl-base](https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/acestep-v15-xl-base)
- For: Pangaea Nights (sleep ambient tracks) and Kahaani Chaos (period suspense score)
- Why: Generates bespoke, copyright-free ambient soundscapes and background beds. Eliminates stock music repetition that triggers reused-content flags during YPP human channel evaluations.
- Test: Deploy a script to Modal free credits to generate a 90-second looping dark ambient drone; evaluate audio quality, loop seams, and credit consumption.
- Risk: MIT license is commercial-ready. Too heavy for 4 vCPU Actions runners; must be executed on Modal or a local Mac.

## [alicangunduz/nefes-0.5](https://huggingface.co/alicangunduz/nefes-0.5)
- For: Kahaani Chaos (secondary cast character voicing in Sannatpur Ki Raatein)
- Why: Supplies distinct vocal timbres and emotional inflection on CPU, allowing audio separation between the Gemini narrator and dialogue cast without relying on expensive external cloning APIs.
- Test: Synthesize 3 distinct dialogue lines on CPU; check memory ceiling under 4 GB and listen for robotic gating or background static.
- Risk: Apache-2.0 license. Early 0.5 release with a small user base; requires strict audio filtering to guard against phonetic clipping.
