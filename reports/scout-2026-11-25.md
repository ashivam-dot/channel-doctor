## [prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2.5-27B-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2.5-27B-GGUF)
- For: Orbitwire and Atlas in Numbers (script drafting and contrast checking on CPU)
- Why: Extremely quantized 27B parameter LLM optimized for CPU inference, enabling richer script storytelling and fact synthesis directly on 4 vCPU runners via llama.cpp.
- Test: Load GGUF weights into llama.cpp on a 4 vCPU runner and measure generation speed (tokens/sec) and script coherence for a sample dataset prompt.
- Risk: Aggressive ternary quantization may cause occasional reasoning degradation; output strict formatting checks are required.

## [huawei-bayerlab/marigold-v2](https://github.com/huawei-bayerlab/marigold-v2)
- For: Orbitwire (depth parallax rendering for trending news photos)
- Why: Provides precise monocular depth estimation for static photos, turning flat image slideshows into engaging 3D depth parallax videos that enhance visual variety.
- Test: Process 3 sample historical news images through Marigold V2 and inspect generated 3D parallax frame outputs for depth artifacts.
- Risk: Diffusion-based depth estimation can be slower on CPU compared to lightweight vision transformers.
