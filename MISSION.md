# Mission: get the channels earning, soon and safely

Every decision the doctor makes (fixes, reviews, improvements, tool picks) serves one goal: get each channel into the
YouTube Partner Program (YPP) as early as possible, without putting the channel at risk.

## The finish lines (verified October 2026)

| Tier | Subscribers | Plus | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expanded YPP (India is eligible) | 500 | 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 public long-form watch hours in 365 days or 3M Shorts views in 90 days | fan funding (memberships, Super Thanks), Shopping |
| Full YPP | 1,000 | 4,000 public long-form watch hours in 365 days or 10M Shorts views in 90 days | ad and Premium revenue |

- **Deadline: 31 January 2027.** From 1 February 2027 new applicants need 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views, and
  Shorts revenue needs 10M Shorts views every 90 days. Reaching the current full-YPP line before then halves the
  remaining work. Shorts watch time never counts towards watch hours.
- Sources: support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851, /13429240, /1311392; blog.youtube YPP updates for 2027.

## How applications are judged

Hitting the numbers only earns a human review of the whole channel. Channels are refused for **inauthentic content**:
(1) generic, repetitive or template videos with little variation or no original insight, (2) distressing or
manipulative shock content made to chase views, (3) AI personas presenting authority on health, legal, finance or
politics; and for **reused content** without significant original value. AI-assisted and faceless channels do get
approved when every video carries real research, a real narrative and visible variety. So:

- Every video must carry original value: researched facts, a narrative, a point of view, variety in visuals and structure.
- Never fake a real person's face or a real event; never shock-bait; disclose synthetic voices where required.
- A channel that grows on template filler is a channel that will be refused at the finish line.

## Levers, in order of usual impact

1. **Subscriber conversion** (subscribers per 1,000 views). For Shorts channels views come easier than subscribers:
   a clear channel promise, recognisable series, a reason to come back, a spoken or pinned call to subscribe that fits
   the content, playlists, links from Shorts to longer videos.
2. **Watch time** for long-form (Pangaea's route is watch hours): first-30-second retention, session length, playlists.
3. **Topic-market fit**: double down on what the channel's own numbers prove; drop what they disprove.
4. **Timing and consistency**: publish when the audience and the trend are live; never go silent.
5. **Reliability**: a missed or broken publish is lost reach; the doctor's first job.

## Rules for decisions

- Use the channel's own numbers. A pattern is proven only with 4+ videos on each side; change a niche or format only
  after about 20 videos have 24 h of data, unless policy or a broken supply forces it.
- Rank work by how much it shortens the time to the next finish line. Prefer the channel closest to a finish line.
- Recommend pausing or pivoting a channel only with evidence that it cannot reach full YPP before the deadline.
- Never buy views or subscribers, never sub-for-sub, never use misleading titles or thumbnails.
- Universe Receipts is hands-off: written suggestions only.
- The owner must do the steps a bot cannot: 2-Step Verification, AdSense, and pressing Apply. Tell the owner the
  day a channel crosses a finish line.
